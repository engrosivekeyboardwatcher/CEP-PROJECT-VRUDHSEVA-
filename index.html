About Vrudhseva

A companion for homes that already care

Vrudhseva (वृद्धसेवा) means elderly care. It is a digital identification and care-support platform for old-age homes and dementia care centres in India. We help teams keep identities, routines, medicines and family links in one calm, accessible place.

🤝

Supports, never replaces

Vrudhseva supports doctors, caregivers, family members and social workers. It does not replace clinical judgement, human affection or professional care.

🪪

Identity with dignity

When memory fades or speech is hard, a person still has a name, a history and people who love them. We help homes hold that identity gently.

🌿

Built for real homes

Large type, low-interaction emergency access, multilingual screens and volunteer-friendly tools — because care work is already demanding.

Field visit · Nagpur

What we learned at Madhurangan Old Age Home

A visit to Madhurangan Old Age Home, Shankarpur, Khapri, Nagpur, showed why identification and care-support must work even when a person cannot recall a name, type on a phone, or speak clearly.

🚓

Wandering without a name

Police sometimes bring wandering dementia patients who cannot recall their name, home or family. Homes still open the door — and then search for identity with very little information.

🤲

Interfaces that exclude

Some residents cannot speak clearly, type, or use touchscreens. Tools must work through staff, QR tags, large buttons and low-interaction emergency access.

💛

Care is more than medicine

Residents need affection, routine and patience, not only medicines. Digital support should protect those human rhythms — not crowd them out.

    From found to family

    How it works

    Identity matches are never automatic end-points. A human always verifies before a family is contacted.

    Person found

    Police, a neighbour or a facility receives someone who cannot be identified.

    Registered

    Photo, distinguishing details and consent notes are recorded with care.

    Searched & matched

    Approved centres search a shared registry for possible matches.

    Human verification

    Staff confirm identity. Algorithms never close a case alone.

    Family contacted

    Known relatives or social workers are reached through approved channels.

    Reunification & care

    The person goes home or continues supported care with a living identity record.

    Learn with kindness

    Dementia myths, facts and gentle tips

    Tap a card to flip myth and fact. These notes are for awareness, not diagnosis.

    Myth

    Dementia is just a normal part of ageing.

    Fact

    Ageing raises risk, but dementia is not inevitable. Changes in memory, language or behaviour deserve medical attention.

    Myth

    People with dementia cannot feel or understand emotion.

    Fact

    Tone, touch and routine often communicate more than words. Affection still lands, even when names do not.

    Myth

    You should always correct a person who is confused.

    Fact

    Gentle redirection and joining their reality often reduces distress more than argument.

    Caregiving tips

    • Speak slowly, face the person, and offer one idea at a time.
    • Keep a familiar daily rhythm for meals, rest and a short walk.
    • Use names, favourite songs and photographs as memory anchors.
    • Rest the caregiver too — exhaustion is not a failure of love.

    Vrudhseva does not diagnose or cure dementia. Always consult qualified medical professionals.

    Trust is a feature

    Privacy & trust

    Identity data is sensitive. Vrudhseva is designed so that access is limited, consent is recorded, and every match is checked by a person.

    Role-based access

    Staff, coordinators, clinicians and volunteers see only what their role requires.

    Consent records

    When a person cannot consent, a guardian, home in-charge or authorised social worker is documented.

    Audit logs

    Lookups, edits and family contacts leave a trail that homes can review.

    Human verification

    All identity matches are verified by trained staff before anyone is contacted.
